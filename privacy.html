Privacy policy

Last updated October 2, 2026.

This OAuth client is used only by its owner to run rclone on their own computer. It reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive.

No data is collected, stored on a server, sold, or shared with anyone. Files move only between Google Drive and the owner's computer. Access tokens stay in a local rclone config file on that computer.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
